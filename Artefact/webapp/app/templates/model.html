Known limitations

  • Single annotator. All labels come from one person, verified by a domain expert but not independently double-rated, so inter-rater reliability is unknown.
  • One farm, one capture setup. Every training image comes from a single light-proof box and a single smartphone. A session-held-out evaluation lost about {{ '%.3f'|format(PERF.grouped_gap) }} macro F1, so expect some loss on new equipment.
  • Star is the smallest class. 68 eggs in total, 11 in the test set, so its confidence interval is wide, 0.55 to 0.94.
  • Star and Network confusions are the expensive ones. They shift the root-cause split by 40 percentage points and reverse the dominant cause in every age class.
  • The root cause is expert knowledge, not measurement. Never validated against hatch outcomes.
  • Not validated for grading or sale decisions. It supports inspection; it does not replace it.
