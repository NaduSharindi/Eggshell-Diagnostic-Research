## What is this study about?

Micro-cracks in hatching eggs are difficult to see during routine inspection, and when one is set
for incubation it usually fails to hatch and can contaminate neighbouring eggs. This research has
built a software tool that examines a candled egg photograph, reports whether a micro-crack is
present, classifies the type of crack, and suggests whether the likely cause lies with shell
quality or with handling.

The tool exists. What is unknown is whether it is **usable and useful to the people who would
actually operate it**. That is what this session is for.

## Why have I been invited?

Because you work as a production or hatchery manager and have practical experience of egg quality
and the decisions that follow from it. Your judgement about whether this tool would help or get in
the way is the thing being measured.

## What will I be asked to do?

One session of about **45 minutes**, at a time and place convenient to you.

- A short explanation of what the tool is for
- Six short tasks using the tool, such as checking one egg and analysing a tray of twenty
- A ten-question usability questionnaire and four questions specific to poultry work
- A short conversation about what you would change

You will be asked to **think aloud** while you work. This feels unnatural at first and that is
normal.

## Is this a test of me?

**No.** The tool is being tested, not you. If you cannot complete a task, that is a finding about
the design and it is exactly what the study needs to capture. There are no wrong answers and
nothing you do will be reported as a personal performance.
